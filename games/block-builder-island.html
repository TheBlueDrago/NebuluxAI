ISLAND

Your own island to build on. Aim with the circle in the middle of the screen, place blocks with a click and take them away with X. Pick from 12 blocks (glass, glowing light, water, lava and more), climb what you build, and switch on fly to build high. Your island saves itself.

Click or E place · X remove · 1-9, 0 or Q pick a block · G fly (Space up, C down) · Right mouse or Shift to aim
