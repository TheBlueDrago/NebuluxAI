FISHING

Walk to the end of a dock (or an ice hole) and cast. When the bobber dips and you see !, hook it fast, then hold to raise the green bar and keep the fish inside it until the meter fills. Sell your bucket at the Fish Market, buy better rods, and open Coral Bay, the Frozen Lake and the Volcano Lagoon for rarer fish.
