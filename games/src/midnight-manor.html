Midnight Manor

MIDNIGHT
MANOR

Eight guests. One of them is secretly the Shadow, sneaking up to freeze everyone into ice. The Detective carries a lantern: shine it on the Shadow to catch them. Every other Guest collects glowing clues, and every 3 clues earns a lantern flash too.

Guest stay together, collect clues, survive
Detective catch the Shadow
Shadow freeze everyone, don't get seen

WASD move · Space jump · Right mouse drag to look · Shift lock the camera
F or Click shine the lantern · E or Click freeze (as the Shadow)
